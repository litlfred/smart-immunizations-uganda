    Requirements from the UNEPI presentation User requirements – SMART Guidelines. They are recorded here as stated, so they can later be mapped to the WHO guide’s functional and non-functional requirements.

    Each row of the two requirement tables below is also a DAK component 8 instance (smart-base FunctionalRequirement or NonFunctionalRequirement), with the same id, in input/fsh/requirements/. The deck states no actor (“as a”) and no benefit (“so that”) for them, so none is recorded.

    Programme

    • Vision: a Ugandan population free of vaccine-preventable diseases.
    • Mission: to ensure that every child and high-risk group is fully vaccinated with high-quality and effective vaccines against the target diseases according to recommended strategies.
    • Goal: to contribute to the overall objective of the Health Sector Development Plan in reducing morbidity, mortality and disability due to vaccine-preventable diseases, so that they are no longer of public health importance.
    • Objectives: increase access and demand for immunization services; ensure availability of potent and effective vaccines; build EPI management capacity at all levels; monitor disease trends and programme performance; formulate policy, standards and guidelines.

    Current challenges (paper-based system)

    High printing costs; delays in service delivery (time consuming); less accurate data; false zero-dose children (ZDC) and immunization under-coverage (IUC); no flexibility; no real-time processes; vaccine accountability.

    Expected benefits of digitization

    Improved data quality; reminders; savings on printing; support for follow-up; identification of real defaulters; improved data use; improved visibility.

    Functional requirements for an electronic immunization registry (EIR)

    Id Requirement Detail
    UG-FR-01 User authentication and access control Username/password, biometric authentication
    UG-FR-02 Patient record management Personal information, vaccination history and demographic data
    UG-FR-03 Vaccine inventory management Batch numbers, expiration dates and current stock
    UG-FR-04 Vaccination scheduling and reminders Scheduling of vaccination appointments and reminders to patients or guardians
    UG-FR-05 Immunization reporting and analytics Reports such as immunization coverage rates, vaccine wastage and adverse event tracking
    UG-FR-06 Adverse event reporting Report and document adverse events following immunization (AEFI)
    UG-FR-07 Integration with health records Integrate with existing electronic health record systems for comprehensive patient health information

    UG-FR-04 is supported directly by the scheduling logic of this guide.

    Non-functional requirements

    Id Requirement Detail
    UG-NFR-01 Interoperability Data exchange
    UG-NFR-02 Security and privacy Adhere to data security standards, encrypt sensitive data, secure data transmission
    UG-NFR-03 Performance and scalability Scalable to accommodate future increases in usage
    UG-NFR-04 Usability and user experience Easy to use for various users
    UG-NFR-05 Compliance and regulation Adhere to relevant regulations and standards
    UG-NFR-06 Training and support Training resources and support to users for effective adoption and usage
    UG-NFR-07 Reliability and availability Access when needed

    Other system features

    Offline mode; parent or guardian portal; multilingual support; vaccine information and education; data migration and legacy system integration; data auditing and logging; mobile application; data quality checks; data sharing permissions; automated reporting to the National Drug Authority (NDA) and the Uganda Virus Research Institute (UVRI).

    Proposed EIR architecture

    Child identification through integration with the birth registration system of the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA / vital registration system), feeding an EIR with a vaccine record module, scheduling and reminders, and data analytics and reporting. Users: health care providers, patients (via app or portal), and district and Ministry of Health staff.
