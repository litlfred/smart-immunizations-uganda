Context from the UNEPI presentation.

### Service points

* **Static:** at the health facility.
* **Outreach:** in the community.
* **School.**
* **Immunity gap approaches:** campaigns, routine, outbreak response (for
  example COVID-19).

### Client flow and integrated services at immunization sites

Immunization sessions are integrated with family planning, early infant
diagnosis, HIV counselling and testing, nutrition assessment, vitamin A and
deworming, postnatal care, and management of adverse events following
immunization (AEFI).

### Data flow

Micro-plan → vaccination card → immunization child health register (ICHR) →
tally sheet → HMIS 105 report → DHIS2 → monitoring chart.

Support activities: data cleaning, data quality assessment, data review
meetings, and RED/REC categorisation.
