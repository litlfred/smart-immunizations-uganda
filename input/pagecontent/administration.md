Routes and sites of administration for child vaccines, from the UNEPI
presentation (*Sites of child vaccines*). Sides are the child's own left and
right as read from the UNEPI illustration; please confirm with UNEPI.

| Vaccine | Route | Site |
|---|---|---|
| Polio (OPV), rotavirus, vitamin A | Oral | Mouth |
| BCG | Intradermal | Right upper arm |
| Yellow fever | Subcutaneous | Right upper arm |
| Measles-rubella | Subcutaneous | Left upper arm |
| Hepatitis B birth dose | Intramuscular | Left thigh |
| DTP-HepB-Hib (pentavalent) | Intramuscular | Left thigh |
| IPV | Intramuscular | Right thigh, 2 cm apart from PCV |
| PCV | Intramuscular | Right thigh, 2 cm apart from IPV |
{:.grid}

The UNEPI material does not give the route or site for the malaria, HPV or Td
vaccines.

In FHIR, route and site are recorded on `Immunization.route` and
`Immunization.site`.
