    Test patients follow the WHO DAK’s own layout: one directory per Logic library, input/tests/plandefinition/<Library>/<Library>.yaml, turned into FHIR test bundles by WHO’s tools/node/makeExample.js (npm run logic:examples; every file is built on each pull request). Each library’s Test Validation expression names, per test patient, the output that should hold, as the WHO libraries do.

    Library Test patients Checks
    IMMZD18SHepatitisBBirthDoseLogic UGHepB01, UGHepB02 birth dose due and expiring at 7 days; complete when given on day 3
    IMMZD18SPolioBOPVPlusIPVLogic UGPolio01, UGPolio02 IPV 1 due at 6 weeks; IPV 2 due at 14 weeks
    IMMZD18SDTPOnTimeStartLogic UGDTP01, UGDTP02 DTP 1 at 6 weeks; no booster after the primary series
    IMMZD18SMeaslesOngoingTransmissionLogic UGMR01 MCV2 due at 18 months
    IMMZD18SMalariaLogic UGMal01, UGMal02 dose 1 at 6 months; dose 4 at 18 months
    IMMZD18SHPVSingleDoseLogic UGHPV01, UGHPV02 girls at 10 years; not boys
    IMMZD18SDTPTdWCBALogic UGTd01–UGTd03 Td 1 at first contact; Td 3 after Td 2; not men

    The CQL is compiled by the IG build (fhirbuild.yml, the WHO path). The WHO DAK toolchain has no runner that executes Test Validation on each change; the WHO checklist runs it by hand. That gap is reported to the platform rather than filled with a script in this repository.
