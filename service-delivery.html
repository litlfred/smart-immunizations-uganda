    Context from the UNEPI presentation.

    Service points

    • Static: at the health facility.
    • Outreach: in the community.
    • School.
    • Immunity gap approaches: campaigns, routine, outbreak response (for example COVID-19).

    Client flow and integrated services at immunization sites

    Immunization sessions are integrated with family planning, early infant diagnosis, HIV counselling and testing, nutrition assessment, vitamin A and deworming, postnatal care, and management of adverse events following immunization (AEFI).

    Data flow

    Micro-plan → vaccination card → immunization child health register (ICHR) → tally sheet → HMIS 105 report → DHIS2 → monitoring chart.

    Support activities: data cleaning, data quality assessment, data review meetings, and RED/REC categorisation.

    Current digital systems for immunization

    System Use
    eCHIS Community registries
    eIDSR Case-based surveillance
    DHIS2 Routine immunization reporting
    EPIVAC Campaigns
    eMR Facility workflows
    ODK-X Cold chain equipment
    eLMIS Logistics (approved)

    Other organisations UNEPI works with: National Drug Authority (NDA), Uganda Virus Research Institute (UVRI), National Medical Stores (NMS) and the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA).
