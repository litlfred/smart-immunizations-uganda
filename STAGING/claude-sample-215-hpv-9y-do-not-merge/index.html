    This implementation guide is a draft adaptation of the WHO SMART Guidelines Immunization implementation guide to the Uganda routine immunisation schedule of the Uganda National Expanded Programme on Immunisation (UNEPI). It is prepared for review with UNEPI and is not an official publication of the Uganda Ministry of Health.

    What this guide adds to the WHO guide

    The WHO guide gives generic, Member-State-adaptable content: data elements, decision logic and scheduling logic for every WHO-recommended vaccine. This guide keeps all of that and adds the Uganda-specific layer:

    • the Uganda routine immunisation schedule and catch-up rules, as published by the Ministry of Health and presented by UNEPI;
    • routes and sites of administration used in Uganda;
    • a line-by-line comparison with the WHO recommendations;
    • computable scheduling logic: the WHO DAK’s scheduling tables, adapted cell by cell in a copy of the WHO DAK workbook and converted with WHO’s own toolchain, with two WHO parameters configured for Uganda, and test patients in the WHO test format;
    • the UNEPI user requirements for an electronic immunization registry and the service delivery and data flow context;
    • open questions where the Uganda sources are silent or ambiguous and a decision by UNEPI is needed.

    Sources

    1. Uganda Ministry of Health, Uganda Routine Immunisation Schedule (poster), attached to issue #1.
    2. UNEPI, User requirements – SMART Guidelines (presentation), attached to issue #1: current immunisation schedule, catch-up schedule, sites of child vaccines, service points, client flow, data flow, current challenges and user requirements for an electronic immunization registry (EIR).
    3. WHO, Immunization implementation guide (smart.who.int.immunizations), and WHO recommendations for routine immunization – summary tables.

    Where the L1 and L2 content is

    The guide follows the SMART Guidelines layers: L1 is the guideline sources and the recommendations taken from them; L2 is the DAK components built on them. Most L2 components are reused from the WHO immunization DAK, as recorded in input/l2/dependencies.yaml.

    layer content where
    L1 The two ingested sources: the MoH schedule poster and the UNEPI deck library: poster, deck
    L1 Authored recommendations, with their mapping to the WHO vocabulary library/l1-recommendation-scenarios/uganda/ (source only, not rendered as a page)
    L2 User-scenario candidates (53) …/uganda/candidates/ (source only)
    L2 Decision logic: scheduling tables, adapted Scheduling logic, Testing, Artifacts; workbook input/decision-logic/
    L2 Personas, business processes, data elements reused unchanged from the WHO immunization DAK
    L2 Functional and non-functional requirements narrative in UNEPI user requirements; not yet authored as Requirements resources
    L2 Indicators WHO indicators reused; the Uganda target-population denominators are not yet authored

    Status

    Draft. Everything marked as an open question needs confirmation by UNEPI before the logic is used for clinical decision support.
