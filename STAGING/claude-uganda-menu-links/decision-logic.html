    The Uganda scheduling logic is the WHO DAK’s own scheduling logic, adapted where the Uganda schedule differs. It is built the way the WHO immunization DAK builds it (IG Starter Kit; litlfred/smart-immunizations tools/antigenchecklist.md):

    1. L2, the workbook. input/decision-logic/IMMZ DAK_decision-support logic.xlsx is a copy of the WHO DAK workbook. Uganda changes only the cells of the scheduling tables (IMMZ.D18.S.*) listed below. Each changed row’s Comments cell names the Uganda L1 statements it rests on (library/l1-recommendation-scenarios/uganda/).
    2. L3, generated. WHO’s converter tools/node/processDTSchedule.js turns each adapted table into a PlanDefinition and its Library (input/fsh/plandefinitions/, input/fsh/libraries/; npm run logic:generate, checked on every pull request).
    3. L3, completed. Each adapted CQL library is WHO’s finished library of the same name, with only the definitions whose cells changed rewritten, each marked // Uganda: with its L1 key. The new Td table’s library is the converter’s skeleton, completed from its pseudocode on WHO’s DTP data elements.

    Every other WHO table is used unchanged from the WHO IG this guide depends on. The full record, one row per table with its decision and evidence, is input/l2/dependencies.yaml.

    Adapted and added tables

    WHO table Uganda change L1
    IMMZ.D18.S.Hepatitis B.Birth dose schedule expires at birth + 7 days C01, C08
    IMMZ.D18.S.Polio.bOPV plus IPV schedule IPV dose 1 at 6 weeks, IPV dose 2 at 14 weeks S03, S07
    IMMZ.D18.S.DTP.On-time start schedule primary series only (6, 10, 14 weeks); booster rows not generated — review pending S04
    IMMZ.D18.S.Measles.Ongoing transmission schedule MCV2 at 18 months, at least 4 weeks after MCV1 S08, P09, C07, C13
    IMMZ.D18.S.Malaria schedule doses at 6, 7, 8 and 18 months S10, P05–P07, P09
    IMMZ.D18.S.HPV.Single-dose schedule girls, at 10 years P10, S13
    UG.IMMZ.D18.S.DTP.Td women of childbearing age schedule (new) Td 1 at first contact, then 1, 6, 12 and 12 months — review pending P11–P15, S14

    Reused unchanged

    BCG, polio birth dose, Hib 3 doses, pneumococcal 3 doses, rotavirus (WHO already expires every dose at 24 months, which is the Uganda rule C09–C11), rubella high-incidence (one dose from 9 months, given as MR1) and yellow fever.

    Configuration

    Two Uganda rules are values of parameters the WHO libraries already expose; they are configured, not forked:

    WHO parameter Library WHO default Uganda L1
    HepBLowerLimitWeeks IMMZD2DTHepatitisBEncounterElements (used by the 3- and 4-dose schedules) 4 6 S04, P02, C03
    HepBBirthDoseAgeLimitDays IMMZD2DTHepatitisBElements (birth-dose eligibility) 1 7 C01, C08

    Not in scope

    Vitamin A and deworming are not vaccines and are outside the WHO immunization DAK (review item 6). Contraindications (IMMZ.D5.DT.*) and the eligibility tables (IMMZ.D2.DT.*) are WHO’s, unchanged. See open questions.
