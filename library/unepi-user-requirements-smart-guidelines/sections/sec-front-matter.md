MINISTRY OF HEALTH
National Expanded Programme 
on Immunization
GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
Vision
 
To ensure that the 
Ugandan population is free 
of vaccine-preventable 
diseases
Mission
Goal
To contribute to the 
overall objective of the 
HSDP in reducing 
morbidity, mortality and 
disability due to vaccine 
preventable diseases, so 
that they are no longer of 
public health importance
To ensure that every 
child and high-risk group 
is fully vaccinated with 
high quality and effective 
vaccines against the 
target diseases 
according to 
recommended strategies
