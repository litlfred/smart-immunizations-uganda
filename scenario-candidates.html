    IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.A02 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give BCG intradermally and yellow fever subcutaneously in the right upper arm so that each dose is given by the correct route at the correct site.     IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.A03 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give measles-rubella subcutaneously in the left upper arm so that each dose is given by the correct route at the correct site. A03: Measles-Rubella (Subcutaneous) to the baby’s left upper arm
    IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.A04 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give IPV and PCV intramuscularly in the right thigh, 2 cm apart so that each dose is given by the correct route at the correct site.     IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.A05 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give the hepatitis B birth dose and DTP-HepB-Hib intramuscularly in the left thigh so that each dose is given by the correct route at the correct site. A05: Hep B Birth dose (Intramuscular) and DPT-HepB-Hib (Intramuscular) to the baby’s left thigh

C01: Hep B BD: As soon as possible after birth, ideally within 24 hours and up to 7 days
    IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.C02 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give BCG as soon as possible after birth so that every child who is behind is brought up to date without invalid or unsafe doses. C02: BCG: As soon as possible after birth
    IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.C03 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give the 6-week doses at 6 weeks or as soon as possible after so that every child who is behind is brought up to date without invalid or unsafe doses. C03: At 6 weeks (or as soon as possible thereafter)
    IMMZ.UG.UNEPI.C04 As a Health worker (HW), during IMMZ.D Administer vaccine, I give the 10-week doses at 10 weeks or as soon as possible after, at least 4 weeks after dose 1 so that every child who is behind is brought up to date without invalid or unsafe doses. C04: At 10 weeks (or as soon as possible thereafter), and at least 4 weeks after dose 1

C05: At 14 weeks (or as soon as possible thereafter), and at least 4 weeks after dose 2
